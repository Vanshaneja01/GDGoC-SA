Our mission is to inspire, educate, and empower undergraduate and postgraduate students through real-world tech workshops, peer mentorship, collaborative open-source sprints, and Google developer technology tracks.

About the Campus Ambassador Program

The GDGoC Campus Ambassador (CA) Program at IIMT University is an elite student leadership initiative designed to bridge classroom academics with real-world developer execution. As a Campus Ambassador, you represent your academic department, collaborate directly with appointed Student Mentors and Faculty Advisors, and spearhead tech initiatives across the campus.

Ambassadors organize hands-on technical bootcamps, drive awareness for Google Solution Challenge, lead Cloud & Gemini AI study jams, and cultivate an active open-source culture. This program offers a proven pathway to build executive project management skills, earn industry credentials, and connect with top practitioners in technology.

Roadmap & Commitments

Ambassador Responsibilities

A structured, progressive checklist outlining your duties throughout the ambassador tenure.

Branch & Department Community Evangelism

Phase 01 • Outreach

Serve as the primary voice of GDGoC within your department. Spread official event updates, share technical resources, and encourage active classroom participation in coding sessions.

Hands-on Lab & Workshop Coordination

Phase 02 • Execution

Coordinate logistical planning and student registrations for workshops focused on Google Cloud, Gemini AI APIs, Web development, and Kotlin/Android labs on campus.

Peer Mentorship & Hackathon Team Formation

Phase 03 • Mentorship

Assist first- and second-year students in mastering Git, setting up developer environments, and forming multi-disciplinary teams for the Google Solution Challenge.

Monthly Syncs & Impact Milestone Reporting

Phase 04 • Sync

Participate in scheduled bi-weekly and monthly status reviews with assigned mentors, presenting branch participation metrics and feedback for ongoing community improvement.

DevFest & Flagship Summit Operations

Phase 05 • Flagship

Collaborate with the GDGoC core leadership team to execute IIMT University's annual DevFest conference, guest tech keynotes, and incubation pitch days powered by IIMTBIF.
