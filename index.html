Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the GDGoC IIMTU Campus Ambassador program.

Who is eligible to become a Campus Ambassador?
Any enrolled student from any year or discipline at IIMT University who is enthusiastic about technology, community building, and leadership is eligible. Prior experience is welcomed but passion and consistency matter most!
What is the weekly time commitment required?
The program is designed to comfortably fit around academic commitments. You typically need 3 to 5 hours per week to participate in mentoring sessions, coordinate lab events, and submit weekly progress reports.
How do I earn points and move up the leaderboard?
Points are awarded for completing tasks assigned in the portal—such as organizing a workshop (+100 pts), successfully verifying participant registrations (+10 pts per student), and submitting on-time weekly reports (+50 pts).
What are the requirements for the official Certificate & LoR?
To receive the Certificate of Completion and Letter of Recommendation, ambassadors must complete their compulsory tenure tasks, reach at least the Silver Tier (1,000 pts), and receive approval from their assigned student mentor.
