GDG on Campus IIMT Meerut
Pioneering Technical Excellence, Student Innovation & Open Source.

Google Developer Groups on Campus (GDGoC) at IIMT University, Meerut is the university’s official student-led developer community, proudly powered by the IIMT Business Incubation Foundation (IIMTBIF).

Our mission is to foster a world-class engineering, coding, and entrepreneurship culture across campus. We empower over 20,000+ students across B.Tech Computer Science, IT, AI & ML, BCA, MCA, and Polytechnic to transition from curious learners into industry-ready software engineers and startup innovators.

Through hands-on workshops in Google Cloud, Generative AI with Gemini, Android Compose, Flutter, and Firebase, our community organizes hackathons like DevHack IIMTU, participates in the global Google Solution Challenge, and facilitates startup incubation directly with IIMTBIF.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the GDGoC IIMTU Campus Ambassador program.

Who is eligible to become a Campus Ambassador at IIMT University?
Any enrolled student from any department (B.Tech CSE, IT, AI & ML, BCA, MCA, or Polytechnic) at IIMT University who is enthusiastic about developer culture, peer learning, and leadership is eligible. Passion and consistency matter most!
What is the weekly time commitment required?
The program comfortably accommodates your academic schedule. Typically, ambassadors dedicate 3 to 5 hours per week for mentoring syncs, coordinating lab sessions, and submitting weekly progress reports.
How do the allotted mentors assist ambassadors?
Your assigned mentor (Disha Agarwal, Aarushi Singh, Akshita Tyagi, Arsh Zehra, or Khyati) conducts regular 1-on-1 reviews, helps plan workshop curriculums, validates task deliverables, and recommends you for certificates and LoRs.
What are the requirements for official Certification & LoR?
To receive the verified Certificate of Leadership and formal Letter of Recommendation from GDG on Campus IIMT and IIMTBIF, ambassadors must complete their tenure tasks and maintain good standing with their mentor.
